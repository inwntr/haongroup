About Haon Group

The Haon Group is a business group focused on innovation, technology, and the development of modern digital solutions. The initiative began with the creation of Haon Technologies, a company dedicated to software engineering, web development, systems, and digital solutions. With a long-term vision, Haon Group aims to build an ecosystem of companies centered on technology, innovation, and new products, continuously expanding its areas of expertise.

Our goal is to develop solutions that connect technology, engineering, and creativity to create a tangible impact in the digital world.

Our History

The Haon Group was born from the vision of creating an ecosystem of companies focused on technology, innovation, and modern digital solutions. It all started with the founding of Haon Technologies, a company dedicated to systems development, digital experiences, and software engineering. As projects grew and the strategic vision expanded, the idea emerged to structure a business group capable of bringing together different areas of technology — from software and automation to hardware and innovation. Today, Haon Group serves as a foundation for creating new companies, products, and solutions that will continuously evolve over the years, always guided by innovation, engineering, and creativity.
